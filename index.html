The goal of Practical Assignment №4 is to extend the textured and normal-mapped analytical model from PA3 by implementing interactive transformation of texture coordinates in the uv-plane. According to the task, even-numbered variants must implement texture rotation. In addition to rotation, the texture center is translated interactively using keyboard input.

The surface is shaded using a per-pixel lighting model with diffuse, specular, and normal maps. Sievert’s Surface is generated analytically from its parametric definition and rendered as a triangle mesh. Rotated texture coordinates are computed in the vertex shader using the center point (uc, vc) and rotation angle θ, forming: 

(u', v') = R(θ) · ((u, v) − (uc, vc)) + (uc, vc)

Keyboard controls allow modifying the texture center:
W / S — move the texture center up / down
A / D — move the texture center left / right

The rotation of the texture is animated over time, demonstrating that the shading and TBN basis correctly respond to dynamic UV transformations. The transformation affects the diffuse, normal, and specular maps simultaneously, producing coherent lighting across all texture channels.

Drag your mouse on the cube to rotate it.
(On a touch screen, you can use your finger.)

Interactive Controls

  • U segments — resolution along parameter u
  • V segments — resolution along parameter v
  • W, A, S, D — move the texture center

This assignment demonstrates the correct application of texture-space transformations and the integration of animated UV rotations into a per-pixel shaded analytical surface model.


Texture center (u, v): (0.50, 0.50)
